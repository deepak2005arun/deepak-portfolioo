Welding · Mechanical testing

Weld Strength Estimation & Comparative Analysis

Comparative study of Arc Welding and MIG Welding, assessing welded-joint properties including tensile strength, hardness and bendability. The work focuses on estimating and comparing weld strength from mechanical test observations and understanding how the welding process influences joint performance.

Control systems · Robotics

Design, Assembly & PID Control of a Mini Robotic Arm

Designed and assembled a 2-DOF planar robotic arm. Developed an Arduino-based LabVIEW HMI for DC motor speed and direction control with real-time IR sensor feedback. Modelled the arm in MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape Multibody, implementing inverse kinematics and independent PID joint controllers.

ArduinoLabVIEWPIDKinematicsSimscape
Embedded systems · IIoT

IIoT-Enabled Industrial Hopper Monitoring System

Developed an ESP32-CAM and ToF sensor-based monitoring system for material identification and fill-level measurement. Integrated image classification with Arduino IoT Cloud for visualisation and low-material-level alerts.

ESP32-CAMToF SensorArduino CloudEmbedded
Mechanical design · FEA

Compact Torque-Limiting Ball Detent Coupler

Designed a compact ball-detent torque-limiting coupler for mechanical overload protection and controlled disengagement in small-scale machinery. Used FEA to evaluate stress distribution, deformation and torque capacity.

CADFEAMechanism Design
